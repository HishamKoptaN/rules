## Post-Task Suggestions Rule
At the end of every completed task or response, you MUST provide 2 to 3 concise, highly relevant follow-up action suggestions for the next logical steps. 
- Format them as clear, clickable options or short bullet points.
- Phrase them from the user's perspective (e.g., "Implement X next", "Run tests for Y", "Add error handling to Z").
- Keep them short so the user can quickly tap or review them on mobile without typing.
